* **Portfolio is to sell** - Every portfolio has one purpose - to sell. The creator compiles their work in portfolio which recruiter uses as a proof to assess the skills and experience and basis that, make a decision to consider the person for hiring. A portfolio that does not sell is worthless
* **Sell the creator** - Personal portfolio should sell the creator, not a product or study. Therefore, focus should remain on the individual
* **Put things that matter** - Portfolio is compilation of work, testimonial and everything that can help a recruiter/ hiring manager develop confidence. Anything which is not serving that purpose should be removed.
* **Leave good impression** - The portfolio in itself should leave a good impression. Essentially it should be good packaging for good product.

### Text
* **Use less text** - Portfolio should not be text heavy. People generally scan a portfolio and text tends to take backstage in that process. Keep only the text that is needed for the story. Cut everything else. Resist the temptation for adding extra context that can possibly confuse the reader.
* **Make sense soon** - Do not make reader wait too long to understand why something is important. It should click
* **Keep the language simple** - Use industry standard jargon judiciously and let every word bear some importance. Do not write generic abstract things.
* **Sound professional** - User crisp polished language that sound confident. Use impactful words but remain honest

### Visuals
* **Visual is the key** - Visuals are the primary elements of a portfolio. Treat them as such
* **Quality matters** - Keep visuals high quality and tasteful. Avoid any errors in detailing.

### Audience
* **Design for ego** - Portfolio at any stage should not hurt the ego of hiring manager. If the hiring manager is part of a compete org, do not bring down their product/ service to contrast yours.
* **Reflect your best behaviour** - Know that hiring manager Hiring managers not only access skills and experience from portfolio but also personality. Your traits like approachable, friendly, humble, polite, confident starts reflecting from portfolio itself.

### UX
* Keep navigation simple but not boring
* Realize that HM would need to visit other portfolios before and after yours. Don't make them learn new things. They shouldn't struggle with navigation.
* People generally look for past work and CV. Keep them available
