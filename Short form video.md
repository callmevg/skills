* Always design for video to be watched with audio off. For videos having talk track, make sure captions are added.
* Primary content stay closer to the center. Avoid staying closer to edges, even for captions
* Dissipate energy. Low energy videos do not perform well. 
* Never take too long to arrive at the point

1. Process video
	* While showcasing the process to make/ build something, start by revealing the end outcome and talking about it without overselling, followed by "that's how I did it" 
	  OR 
	  directly jump into the fun part of process and quickly lead to the video. For process, do not show creator's view. Make sure the process part seems easy to follow and logical. Cut anything which is not helping follow.
	* For the product demo, overlay works better than full screen along with caption. However, process can take entire screen. 
	* While showing process, take multiple shorts from multiple angle/ vantage point/ part of process and join them
	* For process, break down the flow into numbered steps
	* For long process, speed up but find 1-2 parts worth slowing down. Eg. drink break while working out or error while painting.
* In conversation based content, have a text context of conversation in the video itself
2. Travel video
	* When place is remote, hook it with something known like '2hrs from Delhi'.
	* Fix the pace. For event driven trip, pace is faster as compared to relaxing trip.
3. When outcome is the content
	* Add creative part of brief first - 'I was tired of single shade jeans' or 'what if smartwatches are made in Rajasthan'
